Dataset title: Attachment and social anxiety
Authors: Mr. Jacob Lincoln, Dr. Liesbeth Tip, Dr. Sofia De La Fuente Garcia

How to use this file to replicate results: Please refer to the methodology below to replicate recruitment procedures. Use the three instruments listed below to measure Attachment-Related Anxiety, Social Anxiety and Self-Esteem.

Methodology: Participants were recruited via the University of Edinburgh’s online learning portal and social media platforms, as well as through word-of-mouth. A flyer with a link to the study was shared on these platforms along with a standard message explaining the purpose of the study, the inclusion criteria (i.e., must be over the age of 18), and the contact details of one of the researchers. The participants followed a link to the questionnaire, which was created using the Online Surveys platform. Participants responded to 3 questionnaires to measure 1) Attachment-related anxiety, 2) Social anxiety and 3) Self-esteem. They were also asked to report their gender, age group, relationship status and ethnicity. 

1. Experiences in Close Relationships Scale (shortened Attachment-Related Anxiety subscale). This subscale was shortened by only including the nine items that loaded most strongly on the Anxiety subscale in a factor analysis conducted by Sibley, Fischer & Liu (2005).

2. Social Interaction Anxiety Scale.

3. Rosenberg Self-Esteem Scale. 

Vocabulary of abbreviations:
- URN: Unique Reference Number
- SA_1: Social Interaction Anxiety Scale Question 1. 
- AA_1: Experiences in Close Relationships Anxiety Subscale Question 1.
- SEst_1: Rosenberg Self-Esteem Scale Question 1.

Response coding:
Gender: 1=male, 2=female
Age group: 1=18-24, 2=24-34, 3=35-44, 4=45-54, 5=55-64
Relationship status: 1=married, 2=in a relationship, 4=divorced, 5=in a relationship, 6=never been in a relationship, 7=prefer not to say
Ethnicity: 1=white/Caucasian, 2=Asian/Asian British, 5=Other ethnic group, 6=prefer not to say
